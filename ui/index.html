Accounts

Work laptop check

Chatly keeps people at this Mac out of your chats. It can't hide them from a network that decrypts HTTPS or from monitoring software IT installed.

    Erase

    Signs out of both accounts, deletes the encrypted vault and the Touch ID key, and removes your password.

    Sessions are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 under a key derived from your password with Argon2id. Instagram and Facebook run in separate in-memory browser stores and can't reach Chatly itself.
